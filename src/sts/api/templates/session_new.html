Execution assumptions read-only — how orders are simulated
Fill model
Entries fill on the next 5m bar close at close ± half-spread. Stops fill at stop − slippage − half-spread; if a bar gaps through the stop, the full gap loss is taken at the open.
Slippage tiers
Adverse-only: base tier for liquid bars; illiquid/low-volume bars pay base × low-volume multiplier (fail-conservative).
Cost schedule
c1.0.0 — brokerage, STT (sell), exchange txn, SEBI, GST, stamp duty (buy), DP charge (sell). Every component rounded to the paisa; totals balance against the paper ledger.
Data
5-minute bars, IST-naive timestamps; daily candles for context. Entries blocked fail-closed whenever the feed is stale or closed.
